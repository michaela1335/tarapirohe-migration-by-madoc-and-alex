How to Migrate

  • 🕹️ Fly (3D Bird's-Eye View): Use W / A / S / D, Arrow Keys, or the on-screen Movement Circle to steer up, down, left, and right along the braided river.
  • 🪨 Braided River Hazards: Weave around river boulders, gravel banks, and grass islands. Taking hits breaks a Flawless run!
  • 🦅 Black-Backed Gulls (Karoro): Watch out for swooping gulls that chase you across the river channels!
  • 💧 Drink & Eat Minigame: Fly into glowing blue/green river eddies to play a quick timing minigame and restore your migration energy.
  • 🌿 No-Stress Trivia & Upgrades: Answer fun Tarapirohe conservation trivia after each stage to unlock flight upgrades!
